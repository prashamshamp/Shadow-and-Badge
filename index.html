Instructions & Scoring Rules

1. Fresh Cards Every Round

Secret roles are randomly shuffled and dealt to every player at the start of each round. Nobody is eliminated—all players accumulate points across rounds.

2. Sequential Turn Order

Turns proceed strictly in order: Criminal 1/2 $\rightarrow$ Doctor 1/2 $\rightarrow$ Police 1/2. Targeted player selections remain 100% confidential.

3. Confidential Point Matrix

Criminal (+1 Pt) Targeted a Villager.
Doctor (+1 Pt) Healed a targeted Villager.
Police (+1 Pt) Arrested a Criminal.
Villager (+1 Pt) Survived the round.
